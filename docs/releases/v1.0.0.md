## Strata 1.0.0

The first release of Strata, a PSD/PSB viewer for Android with a Material 3 Expressive interface.

**Install:** download `strata-1.0.0.apk` below and open it on your phone. You may need to allow installing apps from your browser or file manager. Requires Android 10 or newer.

### Highlights
- **Opens PSD and PSB** at 1/8/16/32 bits per channel. Supported color modes are RGB, CMYK, Lab, Grayscale, Indexed, Bitmap and Duotone.
- **Exact preview** from the composite Photoshop saves in the file. When a file has no saved composite, Strata renders it from the layers.
- **Layers panel:**
  - the full group tree, with thumbnails
  - visibility toggles that re-render on the device
  - "show only this layer" and one-tap restore
  - per-layer details, including the contents of text layers
- **Own compositor:** all 27 Photoshop blend modes, opacity and fill, layer and group masks, clipping masks, and pass-through and isolated groups.
- **Viewer:** pinch zoom, fling, animated double-tap zoom, sharp pixels with a grid at high zoom, and a checkerboard, white or black backdrop.
- **Export:** save or share full-resolution PNGs, either of what's currently visible or of a single layer.
- **Material 3 Expressive design:**
  - dynamic color, with light and dark themes
  - spring motion and buttons that change shape when pressed
  - a shape-morphing loading indicator
  - a floating toolbar
  - sheets that follow the back gesture
- **Getting files in:** opens files from the file picker, "Open with" and Share. Recent files show thumbnails, and a bundled sample lets you try the app immediately.

### Known limitations
- **Not reproduced:** adjustment layers and layer effects (shadows, strokes, glows). They are included in Photoshop's saved composite but not in Strata's own renders. A "Live render" chip lists what was skipped.
- **Real devices:** this release was checked on the JVM with Robolectric. Please report anything that misbehaves on your phone.
